08 / DOCUMENT LIFECYCLE

From request to cancellation

The exact proposed physical sequence, explained one step at a time.

01

Start from an accepted request

A safety document can only start from a document request that an AP or SAP has accepted for planning. Its details carry across, so nothing is entered twice, and the document keeps the request reference.

02

Prepare and verify

The AP/SAP identifies the Points of Isolation (POIs) on the approved SLD, checks the required dedicated lock numbers and confirms the actual apparatus and safety precautions under site rules. The app is checked for existing document or key conflicts.

03

Collect the isolation keys

The AP/SAP retrieves the relevant keys from Cabinet 1 or 2, matching them to the drawing. If a required key is missing or its custody cannot be reconciled, the document cannot be issued. The hook tag and app record are investigated.

04

Secure the lockout box

The isolation keys are placed into a selected numbered lockout box. The box is secured with its own matching-number lock.

05

Issue and register the document

The application checks the published programme again against the chosen locks and the document’s duration, and any unresolved conflict stops the issue. Once the required safety-rule checks are complete, the AP/SAP issues the appropriate document. As it is issued, the CP fits their personal lock to the box and keeps its key. This is all one process. The AP/SAP records its type, activity, asset, POIs, issuing AP, recipient CP, issue date/time, planned duration, associated keys and box in the app.

06

Return the box and secure its key

The locked box is returned to its designated position in Cabinet 3. The AP/SAP opens Cabinet 4 with their own signed-for key and places the key to the box’s matching lock on its hook, where it stays while the box is in use.

07

Monitor the live document

Authorised users consult the app to identify the recipient and contact details. An overdue document (for example 24 or 36 hours against its allotted duration) triggers a visible alert and follow-up; it is not automatically cancelled. A programme activity can also set a clearance deadline for the document.

08

Cancel and reconcile

After the document is properly returned and cancelled under site rules, the CP removes their personal lock and the AP/SAP releases the box lock in the approved sequence. All POI keys return to their numbered hooks in Cabinets 1/2, the box returns to Cabinet 3 and its lock key returns with it. The app is updated only after physical reconciliation.

Electrical safety rule: The app does not prove isolation, deadness or earthing. Safety documents may only be issued or cancelled by appropriately authorised personnel in accordance with the applicable site rules.
