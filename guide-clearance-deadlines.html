09 / DAILY OPERATIONAL CONTROL

Clearance deadlines

A clearance deadline shows which documents must be reviewed for cancellation before a planned activity can go ahead.

Expired is not the same as a clearance deadline

Expired documentClearance deadline
What it meansThe document has passed its recorded planned duration.The document must be reviewed for cancellation before an upcoming activity.
Where it comes fromThe time recorded when the document was issued.The published daily programme.
What you doContact the recipient and establish the reason.Contact the recipient early enough to meet the target.

Finding the recipient

Open the document from the list to reach the recipient’s details. If a phone number is missing, add it to the CP register so the next person is not left searching.

Documents marked as same network

These are listed for you to check, but they do not count as outstanding. They share a ring or feeder group with the activity.

A deadline never cancels a document.

The application will not cancel a document or show equipment as available because a deadline has passed. Passing a deadline does not allow any lock to be removed. Cancellation stays subject to the safety rules and physical checks.
