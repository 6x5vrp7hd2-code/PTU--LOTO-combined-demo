13 / ASSURANCE

Safety, security and deployment

The physical process and application must both be validated before use on a live electrical system.

Physical operating controls

  • Approved electrical safety rules and roles for APs, SAPs and CPs.
  • Verified numbering and location of all locks, keys, boxes and apparatus.
  • Each authorised AP/SAP signs for and guards their own Cabinet 4 key.
  • Defined response to missing keys, lock discrepancies and lost personal keys.
  • Document issue/cancellation and physical reconciliation procedures.
  • Agreed authority for who reviews requests, publishes the programme and resolves conflicts.
  • An outage procedure and paper fallback for the request and programme stages.

Digital security controls

  • Verified individual user identities and appropriate role-based access.
  • Encrypted data and secure hosting with client-specific separation.
  • Tamper-evident audit records for key/document changes.
  • Reliable shared synchronisation, conflict handling and offline failure procedures.
  • A connection map verified against approved drawings, and retested when they change.
  • A restricted contractor view that hides unrelated documents, drawings and other contractors’ information.
  • Access-approved drawings and protected personnel contact data.
  • Independent security testing, incident response and recovery arrangements.
Proposed, not implemented:

A separate cybersecurity assurance proposal exists for the platform. Its proposed controls require implementation, testing and client acceptance. Neither this guide nor the demonstration establishes compliance or permission for production deployment.
