02 / SITE SETUP

Set up the site

Establish the lock numbers, cabinet arrangement, personal locks and approved drawings before the site comes under safety rules.

RULE 01

Dedicated apparatus locks

Every designated HV and LV switch, circuit breaker and earth switch has its own uniquely numbered dedicated lock. Prior to the system coming under safety rules, each intended lock is fitted to its assigned apparatus and its identification is verified.

RULE 02

Numbered lockout boxes

Every lockout box has a unique number and its own associated lock bearing the same number.

RULE 03

CP personal safety locks

Each CP is issued a uniquely identified personal safety lock after completing CP training. The CP signs for the lock, remains responsible for it and retains its key. Credentials and contact information are entered in the app.

RULE 04

Numbered SLD references

Approved SLDs displayed in the AP office show a small numbered circle at each relevant switch, circuit breaker and earth switch. The circle identifies the dedicated lock assigned to that point.

Before first use:

Verify the drawing revision, the apparatus identification and each lock/number mapping under the approved site procedure. The diagram and app are references, not substitutes for physical verification.
