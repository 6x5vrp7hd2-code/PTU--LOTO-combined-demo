10 / WORKED EXAMPLE

A day on site

One invented example, following a single energisation and a contractor request through the working day.

08:00

Coordination meeting

The Lead SAP learns that Ring 1 is scheduled for energisation at 15:00, and the equipment must be available by 13:00. They enter it and publish the programme.

08:30

Contractor requests a document

A contractor submits a request to work on equipment on Ring 1, from 09:00 to 16:00.

08:31

The application flags a conflict

The requested time overlaps the planned energisation, so the AP office sees a conflict against the request.

09:00

The AP reviews the request

The AP discusses the work with the contractor and the person responsible for coordination. The request is deferred or revised to fit the agreed programme.

11:00

Existing documents are reviewed

Clearance deadlines shows the documents that must be cleared before 13:00. The AP contacts each recipient.

13:00

Clearance target

The AP office checks the status of the documents and isolation arrangements. Any outstanding restriction is escalated.

15:00

Planned energisation

This proceeds only when all required switching, safety-document, isolation, equipment and operational checks have been completed under the applicable safety rules.

The full chain:

Contractor request → AP/SAP review → programme conflict check → isolation planning → safety document issue → live monitoring → document cancellation → equipment reconciliation.

The application provides coordination and visibility.

It does not replace the Control Engineer’s authority or the approved switching process.
