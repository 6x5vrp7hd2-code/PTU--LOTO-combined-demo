05 / DAILY OPERATIONAL CONTROL

The daily programme

One shared view of what must happen today, so nobody issues a document that clashes with what was agreed at the morning meeting.

01

Morning coordination meeting

The Lead SAP or Control Engineer attends and agrees the main requirements of the day, such as energisations and isolations needed for commissioning.

02

Enter the activities

Each activity records the equipment, the scheduled time, who is responsible, how long the equipment stays restricted, and a clearance deadline where documents must be cleared first.

03

Publish

The entries stay in draft until they are published. The AP office sees only the published revision, and the programme checks use only the published revision.

04

Republish when the plan changes

Plans move during the day. Each change is published as a new revision, with the name and time recorded.

What each planning status means

StatusMeaning
Available for assessmentNo programme restriction or active document is recorded.
Reserved for commissioningA planned commissioning or testing activity may stop the equipment being released.
Planned isolationAn isolation is scheduled.
Planned energisationThe equipment is needed for energisation.
RestrictedA restriction is recorded. Release needs it to be reviewed.
Under active safety documentA safety document is recorded against this equipment.
These are planning indicators, not electrical states.

“Available for assessment” does not mean safe, dead or free to use. It only means nothing is recorded. Equipment is always confirmed physically under the site rules.

If today’s programme has not been published:

The application shows a warning and treats checks as not done. An empty programme must never be read as a clear day.

Finding the programme

Open Daily programme from the menu. Tap any activity to see its equipment, deadline, linked documents and related contractor requests.

If the system is unavailable

The programme can be printed. The site needs an agreed outage procedure before live use, and the paper copy must show its revision.
