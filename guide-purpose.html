01 / PURPOSE

Why the system exists

Reduce time lost tracing isolation keys, lockout boxes and safety-document recipients, and keep the AP office aligned with the day’s plan.

The problem

On large data-centre projects, multiple APs can work across the same electrical system. Keys, lockout boxes and live documents may become difficult to locate. The resulting searches can delay planned work, switching and energisation.

The proposed answer

Standardise the physical locations and identification of locks and keys, then link their custody to the document and the responsible AP/CP in an accessible application.

The coordination problem

Requests can reach the AP office with too little information, and the priorities agreed at the morning meeting may not reach every AP. A document could then be issued on equipment needed for commissioning later that day.

The added controls

Every document now starts with a reviewed contractor request, and all APs and SAPs share a published daily programme showing deadlines and equipment that cannot be released.

Three elements working together:

1. A defined, physical LOTO and key-custody system. 2. A supporting digital register for people, assets, keys, lockout boxes and documents. 3. A controlled route for requests and a shared daily programme.
