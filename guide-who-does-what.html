04 / ROLES

Who does what

Everyone in the process has a defined part. Knowing where yours starts and stops is the first thing to learn.

REQUESTS

Contractor

Submits a document request with the equipment, work, RAMS reference, proposed recipient and required times. Sees their own requests and the AP office comments. Replies if more information is needed.

RECEIVES THE DOCUMENT

Competent Person (CP)

Receives the safety document, keeps the working party within its limits, and is responsible for their own numbered personal lock and its key.

REVIEWS AND ISSUES

Authorised Person (AP)

Reviews requests within their authorisation, plans isolations, issues and cancels safety documents, keeps the keys, boxes and records reconciled, and guards their own signed-for Cabinet 4 key.

REVIEWS AND ISSUES

Senior Authorised Person (SAP)

Carries out the AP duties within their authorisation. The site decides any further responsibilities. Being a SAP does not automatically make someone the Lead SAP.

PLANS AND COORDINATES

Lead SAP or Control Engineer

Attends the morning coordination meeting, publishes the daily programme, and is the only role that can record a resolution to a programme conflict.

VIEW ONLY

Management

Can view status and records. A management position does not give electrical authority or the right to change a record.
