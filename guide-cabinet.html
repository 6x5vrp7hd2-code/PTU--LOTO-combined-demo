03 / PHYSICAL CUSTODY

The four-cabinet system

Four LOTO cabinets are located together so keys and lockout boxes always have designated positions.

HV KEYS

Cabinet 1

HV

Dedicated HV isolation-lock keys, each on its numbered hook.

LV KEYS

Cabinet 2

LV

Dedicated LV isolation-lock keys, each on its numbered hook.

LOCKOUT BOXES

Cabinet 3

LB

All numbered lockout boxes, each returned to its assigned position after use.

BOX-LOCK KEYS

Cabinet 4

AP

The safety-lock key of each lockout box while that box is in use.

Your Cabinet 4 key

Each AP and SAP signs for their own copy of the key to Cabinet 4. It is theirs and they must guard it. Management status alone does not confer access: anyone without the required AP/SAP authorisation is excluded.

Missing key hook

If an isolation key is removed, a tag is left on its numbered hook indicating where it has gone and why. This is a physical courtesy indicator; the full record is held in the application. A missing key with no valid reconciliation prevents the proposed document issue.

Key distinction:

The Cabinet 4 key opens Cabinet 4 only. It is not a universal isolation-lock key and must never be used as a bypass of individual isolation or personal-lock control.
